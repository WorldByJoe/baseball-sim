How to judge. Every provisional file below is playable here (open this page from the sounds/ folder so the relative paths work). They were picked without listening: from the recordists’ descriptions, the loudness curve, and an automatic audio classifier (which flagged music and speech). So please audition them; the orange notes say what to listen for. Under each sound, “Further candidates” are other recordings with a licence checked on their own page; their previews stream from Freesound.

How to swap a file.

  1. Open the candidate’s source page and download the original (Freesound needs a free login; the files here were made from the HQ previews, so an original is also an upgrade). Only CC0 or CC-BY (Attribution, not NonCommercial) are allowed, or BBC RemArc.
  2. Put it anywhere, e.g. sounds/incoming/, and run the same chain used here: 
    cd sounds
# one-shot: INPUT SOUND shot OUTPUT [START_S] [MAX_LEN_S] [FADE_OUT_S]
./process.sh incoming/my_cheer.wav cheer_small shot crowd/cheer_small_01.ogg 0 6 0.8
# bed loop: INPUT SOUND bed OUTPUT [START_S] [LOOP_LEN_S]
./process.sh incoming/my_walla.wav bed_normal bed beds/bed_normal_04.ogg 30 60
    It prints the duration, the measured LUFS and what it did.
  3. Update that file’s entry in manifest.json (dur, lufs, source, author, licence, processing) and, for CC-BY, add the credit to CREDITS.md.

Loudness: two-pass (measure, then linear gain) to −23 LUFS for beds and −18 LUFS for one-shots, peaks held at −1 dBFS with at most 6 dB of limiting, so very peaky clips read a little lower. Bed loops crossfade their last 2 s into the start, so they loop without a click.
