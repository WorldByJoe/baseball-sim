What should decide it

Martinez's strikes against the most patient lineup in baseball

The Brewers drew walks on 10.6% of plate appearances, the most of any team (league 8.9%). Martinez walked 3.6% of batters, fewer than 99% of pitchers who threw 1,500+ pitches, and struck out only 15%, so tonight turns on balls in play. On sinkers, changeups and cutters under 93 mph, the Brewers' starters made .345 xwOBA against the league's .322; Turang, Mitchell, Bauers and Contreras were all above .370.

Misiorowski's velocity against the hardest lineup to strike out

Misiorowski struck out 38% of batters at 99.7 mph, top 1% on both. The Rays struck out 18.7% of the time, the fewest of any team. Against fastballs at 97+ mph their lineup missed on 18% of swings (league 21%); Simpson missed on 2% and Hicks on 7%. If they put his fastball in play early, the Brewers' edge narrows.

Fly balls and two big bats

Martinez gave up fly balls (ground balls only 14th percentile) but little hard contact (94th percentile). Bauers and Mitchell swing faster than 96% and 99% of hitters. Home runs separated the Rays' wins from their losses all year.

The middle innings

Both teams' starters averaged about 20 batters a start. In their losses, both bullpens allowed over 3 runs a game, against about 1 in their wins.
