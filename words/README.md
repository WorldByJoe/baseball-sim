# At-Bat Word Tree

Every 2026 regular-season plate appearance as a word in the pitcher's alphabet (B ball, S called strike, W swinging
miss, F foul, f foul with two strikes, C contact, H hit by pitch), one letter per pitch. The 500 most common words
(84% of plate appearances) drawn as a 3D Sankey tree in three.js: each branch as thick as the share of plate appearances
that take it, and drawn toward the corner of how they ended (strikeout, walk or hit by pitch, out in play, base hit).

Built 2026-10-19 from the forecast branch (forecast/words/sankey3d/: tree_data.py, word_tree_3d.src.html); this page is
that build wrapped as a standalone page. Linked from the Couch Analyst (live/).
